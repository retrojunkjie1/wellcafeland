# WELLNESSCAFE — MASTER VISION BLUEPRINT

Version: 1.0  
Status: Architectural Constitution  
Owner: WellnessCafe  

--------------------------------------------------

# 1. SYSTEM PURPOSE

WellnessCafe is a **voice-guided recovery sanctuary and crisis navigation network** designed to support people experiencing emotional distress, addiction recovery challenges, trauma, panic, isolation, and life instability.

The system provides:

• emotional stabilization  
• addiction recovery guidance  
• crisis navigation  
• access to real-world support resources  
• connection to providers and mentors  
• community support routing  
• optional spiritual reflection  

The platform must always remain available as a **compassionate digital presence**, even when:

• authentication fails  
• AI services are temporarily unavailable  
• internet connectivity is weak  

Support must **never be blocked by login requirements**.

--------------------------------------------------

# 2. EXPERIENCE VISION

The system must feel like:

a calm intelligent guide.

It must never feel like:

• a chatbot  
• a documentation interface  
• a complicated menu system  

Primary interaction modes:

• voice guidance  
• audio sessions  
• video guidance  
• minimal text  

Text is secondary.

Audio and visual guidance are primary.

--------------------------------------------------

# 3. CONTENT DEPTH MODEL

WellnessCafe supports multiple depths of guidance.

Level 1 — Stabilization  
30 seconds – 3 minutes  
Used during panic, cravings, or overwhelm.

Level 2 — Guided Sessions  
5 – 15 minutes  
Used for grounding, emotional regulation, reflection.

Level 3 — Learning Content  
10 – 30 minutes  
Educational recovery content.

Level 4 — Deep Programs  
30 – 60 minutes  
Structured healing programs and courses.

# 5. CORE SYSTEM ENGINES

The intelligence of the platform is powered by internal engines.

--------------------------------------------------

COMPASSION ENGINE

Controls the emotional tone of the AI.

Modes:

Stabilize  
Contain  
Rebuild  
Learn  
Navigate  

The engine determines:

• response length  
• voice pacing  
• emotional tone  
• tool suggestions  
• escalation behavior  

Guardrails:

• no shame language  
• no blame  
• no overwhelming explanations during distress  

--------------------------------------------------

RECOVERY TIMELINE ENGINE

Tracks recovery signals over time:

• mood check-ins  
• cravings  
• sleep patterns  
• tool usage  
• relapse events  
• stress signals  

Creates a recovery timeline used to personalize future support.

--------------------------------------------------

CONTEXT MEMORY ENGINE

Stores meaningful personal context such as:

• triggers  
• preferences  
• goals  
• previous successful coping tools  

This allows the guide to respond with continuity and familiarity.

--------------------------------------------------

EXPERIENCE ENGINE

Interprets user input and selects the most appropriate intervention.

Responsibilities include:

• emotional state detection  
• crisis signal detection  
• routing to stabilization tools  
• routing to audio/video guidance  
• routing to providers or resources

--------------------------------------------------

CRISIS ESCALATION ENGINE

Detects high-risk situations such as:

• suicidal ideation  
• overdose risk  
• severe withdrawal  
• extreme panic  

The engine decides when to escalate from AI guidance to real-world assistance.
