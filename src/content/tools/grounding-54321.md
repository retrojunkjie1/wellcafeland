### Reconnect with the room

Noticing one thing around you may help bring attention back to the present. This is an optional coping pause, not a treatment or a test. Stop or choose another kind of support if it feels uncomfortable.

- **Look:** Notice a still object or color.
- **Listen:** Notice a familiar sound—or quiet.
- **Touch:** Hold a familiar object, if you want.
- **Adjust your space:** Change the light, sound, or where you sit.

Choose one for a brief pause. There is no score or required feeling. The app records only that the practice was completed and how long it was open; it does not record the choice or a personal reflection.
