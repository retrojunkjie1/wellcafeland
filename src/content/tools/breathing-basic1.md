### Breathing Reset — 4·6 Breathing

**When to use this:**  
When your chest feels tight, your thoughts are racing, or you feel like you "can't catch your breath."

**How to do it:**

1. Breathe in gently through your nose for a count of **4**.

2. Exhale slowly through your mouth for a count of **6**.

3. Repeat for **10–15 breaths**.

4. Notice what shifts in your body without forcing anything.

You're not trying to "fix" yourself.  
You're giving your nervous system a softer rhythm to follow.
